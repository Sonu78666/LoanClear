How to Part-Prepay Your HDFC Home Loan (Step by Step)

A lot of HDFC home loan borrowers hit the same wall: NetBanking doesn't have an obvious part-prepayment option, and there's no clear official page walking through what to do next. Here's the actual process, based on a real prepayment request that went through in October 2026.

Note: this reflects one borrower's experience and HDFC's process can change or vary by loan type/branch. Treat the email address and steps below as a starting point, and verify current details with HDFC directly if anything looks different for your account.

Step 1: Check NetBanking first

Log in to HDFC NetBanking (or the mobile app) and look under your home loan account for a prepayment or "part payment" option. Some accounts do have this available directly online. If you don't see it, or the option is unclear/doesn't let you specify reduce-tenure vs. reduce-EMI, move to email.

Step 2: Email HDFC's loan customer care

Send a request to customer.service@hdfcbank.bank.in — note the domain is hdfcbank.bank.in, HDFC's verified banking domain, not the more common hdfcbank.com. Double-check this against HDFC's own NetBanking/app contact page before sending, since bank email domains are exactly the kind of detail worth verifying yourself.

Include in the email:

  • Your loan account number
  • The exact prepayment amount
  • Whether you want the amount applied to reduce tenure or reduce EMI — HDFC won't guess this for you, and the default handling may not be what you want
  • A request for the updated amortization schedule once the prepayment is processed

Example email

Subject: Home Loan Part Prepayment Request – [Your Loan Account Number] Dear HDFC Bank Team, I would like to make a part prepayment on my home loan account. Loan Account Number: [your loan account number] Prepayment Amount: Rs. [amount] Prepayment Type: Part Prepayment (Reduce Tenure) — I want this amount applied toward reducing the loan tenure, not reducing the EMI amount. Please process this request and share: 1. The updated loan amortization schedule after prepayment 2. Details on how to proceed with the payment (online) Thank you. [Your name] [Your registered mobile number]

Step 3: Wait for a reply with a payment link

HDFC typically replies within 2–3 business days with a payment link for the prepayment amount.

Step 4: Pay within 1 day — the link expires

This is the detail that trips people up: the payment link is usually valid for only 1 day. If you don't complete the payment within that window, you'll likely need to send a fresh request and wait another 2–3 days for a new link. Set aside time to pay as soon as the link arrives rather than leaving it for later.

Step 5: Confirm the updated schedule

After payment, the prepayment typically reflects in your loan account — with an updated amortization schedule — within another 2–3 days. Check your NetBanking account or the statement HDFC sends to confirm the outstanding principal and revised tenure match what you expected.

Decide your prepayment amount and whether to reduce tenure or EMI before emailing HDFC, since that's one of the two things you have to specify upfront. Run the numbers on LoanNiti's planner first so you're requesting with a clear target in mind rather than guessing.
